Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

  • NoxCut edits your videos on your computer: it doesn't upload your videos or your audio to the Internet.
  • We only receive data when you write to us (the feedback box, the support chat or the website form) and the minimum needed for your licence to work.
  • We don't sell data, there are no ads, and neither the program nor the website uses cookies or analytics.

1. Who is responsible

Garzanox, creator of NoxCut. For anything about your data: garzanox.business@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect and why

Your licence. The name you choose, the licence number and your computer's code ("NXC-…": a summary of Windows' or the Mac's internal identifier that can't be reversed; it identifies the computer, not you). They're inside your key and in Garzanox's licence register, on their computer. They're used to activate NoxCut (one licence per person and computer) and to revoke a licence if it's misused. The public list of revoked licences only contains numbers. Legal basis: the licence agreement.

The feedback box (inside NoxCut, only if you send something): your username, the type (idea, bug or something else), the title, the text, any images you attach, your licence number, the NoxCut version and whether you want to appear in the release notes. It's used to read you, reply to you and improve NoxCut. Note: screenshots show whatever was on your screen; check them before sending. If you choose to have your name shown, it will appear in the public release notes (in the program, the website, Discord and GitHub). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in handling what you send us and improving the program; appearing in the release notes, your consent (you can withdraw it by writing to us).

The support chat (only some licences): the text, images and files you send, your licence number and name, and the NoxCut version. It's used to help you with your problems. Legal basis: the licence agreement.

The website form: your name or nickname (if you give it), where to reply to you (email, Discord, Instagram…), the reason, your computer's code (if you give it), the message and the language. It's used to reply to you and, if you want to try NoxCut, to give you a licence. Your IP address is only used to stop spam, stored as a summary that can't be reversed and deleted after 2 hours. Legal basis: your request.

Automatic connections of the program, without sending anything of yours: checking for a new version (can be turned off in Options), checking the list of revoked licences and downloading the list of trending hashtags (all three from GitHub), and downloading the components and AI models the first time (from PyPI, python.org, Hugging Face and GitHub). Those services see your IP address, like any website you visit, and handle it under their own policies.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics, and its fonts are served from the website itself. GitHub may log your IP for security.

What you edit in NoxCut (your videos, audio, projects and subtitles) stays on your computer: transcription, the AI voices and the other features work offline.

3. Who helps us (providers)

What you send us is read by Garzanox and their editor. To store and manage it we use these services:

  • Cloudflare (Workers and Workers KV): stores the feedback, the support chat and the form messages.
  • Discord: each new piece of feedback is notified in a private Garzanox channel (with its text, the username and the first image). Form and support messages don't go to Discord.
  • Anthropic (Claude): feedback, form messages and the support chat may be processed with Claude, an AI assistant, to understand them and prepare replies and fixes.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): hosts this website, the NoxCut downloads and the lists the program checks.

These services belong to US companies, so there are international transfers of data to the United States, with the safeguards the GDPR requires. According to their own privacy policies: Cloudflare, Discord and GitHub take part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Cloudflare and GitHub also use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses), and Anthropic uses the standard contractual clauses.

In addition, NoxCut downloads its components and AI models directly from Hugging Face, PyPI (Python Software Foundation) and python.org. We don't send them any data of yours: they only see your IP address when downloading, like any website you visit, and handle it under their own policies.

5. Your rights

You can ask at any time, by writing to garzanox.business@gmail.com, to see the data we have about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, take it with you (portability) or withdraw your consent. We'll reply within one month at most. We may ask for something to check it's you (for example, your licence number).

If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or to the data protection authority where you live.

6. Minors

If you're under 14, ask your parents or guardians for permission before sending us any data.

7. Changes

If anything in this policy changes, the date above will change; if it's important, we'll also say so in NoxCut's release notes.
