From a long stream to your shorts, with subtitles.

NoxCut is a horizontal or vertical video editor made for streamers: word-by-word subtitles, your camera over the game, your matches and best plays found for you and hundreds of effects. All on your computer, with no cloud and no subscriptions.

What it does for you

The parts of editing a stream that take the most time, NoxCut does by itself; you decide the result.

Word-by-word subtitles

Generated on your computer, without internet, every word with its timing. Fix them by typing in the table, search them like a search engine («where did I say “what a play”?») and pick a shorts style from a gallery: karaoke, word by word, with «pop»…

From horizontal to vertical with one button

The vertical template puts your camera on top and the game below, or the floating camera over it, round and with a shadow. Drag it in the preview; every piece can have its own framing.

Your matches and plays, found for you

A stream of almost three hours: NoxCut finds every match in seconds and moves each one to its own tab. In tactical shooters it also gathers your kills by round: doubles, triples, aces…

Edit by talking

Say it like you would to a person: «remove the silences», «keep only match 3». NoxCut shows what it understood, step by step, and does it in front of you.

And also

Hundreds of effects

Transitions, zooms, filters, animations and over 150 sounds, to drag onto a piece.

Texts and stickers

Animated texts, over 100 letter effects, our own stickers and GIFs.

Uses your graphics card

Export and generate subtitles faster with your GPU, laptops included.

One tab per match

Sequences to work on each part separately and export them all at once.

Several voices

Split each person who speaks onto their own track, with their own subtitles.

Description and hashtags

When exporting it suggests the text for each network.

The whole program, and the subtitles, in both languages.

Editing by voice

You tell it like you'd tell a person: NoxCut shows what it understood, step by step, and does it in front of you. 0:26

Word-by-word subtitles

Generated on your computer (offline), each word with its timing. Corrected by typing, and the style is chosen from a gallery: karaoke, word by word or with “pop”. 0:30

Vertical template with the floating camera

The game full screen and your camera on top, round (or square, 4:3, 3:4…), with a shadow. Drag it anywhere in the preview and the mouse wheel changes its size. 0:26

Finding matches and one sequence per match

A 2 h 40 min stream: NoxCut finds the 11 matches and puts each one in its own tab. 0:24

Collecting your kills

Without typing anything: NoxCut recognises the player from their own deaths, counts the kills in each round and keeps the ones you choose (doubles, triples, aces…). Before changing anything, it says what it's going to do. 0:41

Your projects when it opens

A single window: NoxCut opens with all your projects, each with a frame from its video. Search as you type and, when you choose one, the same window switches to the editor. 0:23

Media: drag and search

Images and GIFs dragged from File Explorer or the Finder to anywhere in the window. With hundreds, the search box finds them as you type; then, into the video. 0:17

The guide: every part, explained in place

“📖 Guide”: choose a topic (for example, “How to make cuts”) and NoxCut takes you to that part, with an explanation next to each button. Or the full tour, which you can stop and continue whenever you like. 0:26

Selecting, deleting and grouping clips

Like in any editor: drag to select several clips, Shift + click, delete a section from every track using the ruler and group clips into a block that moves as one. 0:25

How to start

  1. Download NoxCut and install it.
  2. When it opens it shows you your computer's code (NXC-…).
  3. Send it to us with the contact form and we'll reply with your key.
  4. Paste it into NoxCut and you're done: from then on it updates itself.

On Mac: unzip the .zip, drag NoxCut to Applications and, the first time, open it with right-click › Open.

About us

NoxCut is made by Garzanox, a shooter streamer, to edit Garzanox's own streams. It started as a tool to get subtitles for those clips and has grown with what the editor and the community asked for.

Every idea comes in through the program's own suggestion box and many ship in the next version: there's something new almost every day.
